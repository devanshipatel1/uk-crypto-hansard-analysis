# Corpus Inclusion and Exclusion Protocol

## Study window

1 January 2020 to 31 December 2025.

## Unit of analysis

Individual spoken parliamentary contribution.

## Included venues

- House of Commons
- Westminster Hall
- House of Lords
- relevant substantive committee proceedings where spoken debate is available

## Retrieval dictionary

High-precision and contextual terms include:

- cryptoasset / crypto asset
- cryptocurrency / crypto currency
- Bitcoin
- Ethereum
- stablecoin
- blockchain
- digital asset
- distributed ledger
- DLT
- crypto exchange
- crypto regulation

## Inclusion rule

Retain a contribution when the text substantively concerns cryptoassets, token markets, blockchain-based financial activity, or regulation/governance of those activities.

## Exclusion rules

- blockchain references unrelated to financial or cryptoasset policy;
- generic uses of “token” or “digital asset” with no cryptoasset relevance;
- CBDC-only discussion unless the contribution explicitly compares CBDCs with private cryptoassets or stablecoins;
- purely procedural text with no substantive policy content;
- duplicates;
- written-only material where the study is defined as spoken parliamentary discourse.

## Metadata preserved

- date/year;
- House/venue;
- debate title and debate ID;
- speaker name/ID where available;
- party where available;
- full contribution text;
- matched retrieval term(s);
- word count;
- source URL.

No synthetic or invented parliamentary records are inserted to fill missing years.
